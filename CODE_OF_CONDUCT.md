# Code of Conduct

## Where this applies

This applies to issues, to issue comments, and to every other space of this
project.

## The standard

Discuss the work. Report the defect, give the evidence, and say what you expect
instead. Disagree about the content as sharply as the content deserves.

Do not insult a person. Do not harass a person. Do not post someone's private
information. Do not derail a thread with unrelated material. Do not post an
advertisement.

## Enforcement

One person maintains this repository, so enforcement is one person's judgment.
I can edit, lock, transfer, or delete any content here. I can block an account.

I do not promise a committee, an appeal, or a response time. If that is not
enough process for you, do not post here.
